Why I made this

Apollo. Phoebus. The Delian God. The son of Latona. Same god, four names, and that's just one page of Ovid.

I kept stopping to look things up, so I built the friend I wanted beside me while reading: one who knows everyone's names, families and haunts.

What it does

Who's who

Tap any name for a profile: every name they go by, their family, their big moments.

Family trees

See how everyone's related, for the part you're reading right now.

Maps

Where each scene happens, from Troy to the edge of the underworld.

Notes beside the text

Odd words, allusions and the translator's own footnotes, a tap away.
